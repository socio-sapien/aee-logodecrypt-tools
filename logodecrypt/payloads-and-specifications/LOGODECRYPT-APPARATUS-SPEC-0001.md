# LOGODECRYPT-APPARATUS-SPEC-0001 — THE THAUMATURGICON: Omission-Distance Apparatus (desk layer, v1)

Classification: AEE operational specification. Date of record: 2026-10-26. Owner: Nathaniel Jaffe Norris, THAUMATURGICON VIRION PRIMAGENATOR. Implementation: TOOL-0002, AEE-STRUCTURAL-DISCLOSURE-AUDITOR-v1.1.html.

## 1. Purpose

The Thaumaturgicon illuminates the silence between the words: it renders absence measurable. For any given text it measures the structural distance between three surfaces: the UNSAID (omission surface), the MEANT (commitment structure), and the DONE (executed operative statements). It does not read minds. It measures structure. Every metric below is a transparent, documented proxy computed at desk layer.

## 2. Definitions and formulas

2.1 Genre detection. Keyword scoring selects one frame: privacy / data disclosure, license / terms grant, announcement / launch, or general disclosure.

2.2 Disclosure frame. A genre-specific checklist of categories that a complete disclosure of that genre would ordinarily address on-page.

2.3 Omission distance (the unsaid). Silent categories divided by frame size. A category is present when its documented detection pattern matches the specimen text; otherwise silent. Absence is a structural observation of the specimen text, not an allegation about the source institution: the category may be addressed off-page, and the apparatus does not claim otherwise.

2.4 Commitment ladder (the meant). Institutional verb phrases classify as binding (must, shall), procedural (take steps, steps, work to, seek to, aim to, strive to, endeavor to, committed to, efforts to, measures to), or permissive (may, might, could, can bound to an institutional actor).

2.5 Commitment distance. (procedural plus permissive) divided by (procedural plus permissive plus binding). The share of institutional verb phrases that assert meaning without binding the actor.

2.6 Unfalsifiable completion pair. A procedural verb followed within sixty characters by an outcome verb (minimize, protect, ensure, prevent, reduce, safeguard, mitigate, limit) with no completion predicate. The structural signature of outcome asserted by process.

2.7 Executed operative statement (the done). A sentence with an institutional subject and a present-factive operative verb, containing no procedural qualifier, no modal, and no desk hedge.

2.8 Execution distance. Procedural divided by (procedural plus executed). The share of meaning not backed by executed structure. Zero when the denominator is zero.

2.9 Negation window. Token distance from a carve-out token (unless, except, other than) to the nearest preceding operative verb within 120 tokens. Beyond that bound the carve-out is unbound and is flagged, not measured. Wider windows bury the silence farther from the grant it qualifies.

## 3. Discipline

INSTRUMENT and DESK readings are never conflated. PIC and certified hedge determination remain outputs of the live chassis scorer. All Thaumaturgicon lexicons are transparent and inspectable in the build. Frames are bounded to the specimen text.
