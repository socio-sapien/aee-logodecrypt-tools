THE REVEAL IS THE BOUNDARY
A powerful review is not a verdict.
PRIME's demonstrated strength here is narrow and legible: preserve a string, expose selected text cues, compare number multisets and curated polarity pairs, make certain Unicode features visible, label generated transformations, and create a deterministic local receipt.
What it can showLiteral pattern matches, offsets, selected number/polarity differences, checklist cue matches, script/control observations, and exact-input receipts.
What it cannot establishIntent, truth, authorship, semantic equivalence, legal meaning, actual exploitability, market performance, or a system-wide causal path.
Where human review mattersRole-bound numbers, paraphrase, omitted context, source identity, decision authority, and any external consequence.
The differentiator to evaluate: one source-linked workflow for exact cues, bounded comparisons, generated alternatives, and explicit unknowns. Validate it with independent users and traces before making market or efficacy claims.
