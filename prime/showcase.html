Input integrity · bd3ff4c2b943fb059cb07ba80d8eece55e081ffda7acded7d18a78b5f9dbdea0

Useful because the boundary is visible.

This is an experimental toolkit, not a truth detector, mind-reader or security certification. A missing cue is scoped to the supplied excerpt and checklist. A generated alternative is never evidence about what an author secretly meant.

The standard-library module makes no network calls. Its source/candidate checks are narrow and can miss role swaps or paraphrases. The letter layer uses stipulated associations, not historical WordNet/cache charges. Hash receipts are unsigned and do not establish identity or independent validation.

No real corpus records or personal evidence are included. No customer demand, integrations or market exclusivity are claimed.

python aee_logodecrypt_prime.py --demo
python aee_logodecrypt_prime.py --file candidate.txt --context source.txt
python aee_logodecrypt_prime.py --self-test
